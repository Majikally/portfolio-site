After doing a PageSpeed Insights test, I was left with an accessibility score of 93.

The only thing the tool highlighted as being bad was contrast. Specifically, when I had emphasis on certain words in my project sections. White didn't look great with a very light blue.

I used the WebAIM Contrast Checker to figure out what color I should change <strong> elements to. Eventually, I settled for a darker blue/violet. #1226BA

The footer also could use some reworking, as the white text on a green background was a little hard to read. I changed the background to be a darker green (215E41) as the footer should ideally be darker anyways.
